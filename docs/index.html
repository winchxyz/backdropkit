Animated themes for Claude Code

What is this?
winch-ai

BackdropKit is a set of animated pixel-art themes for the Claude Code desktop app. A theme plays behind your conversation, the way this page plays behind me, and the app's panels take their colour from it.

12 of the 14 follow your clock, from night to sunrise, day and sunset. They are made by winchxyz, and they are free and open source.

How do I install one?
winch-ai

Download the archive, then give Claude Code this prompt. It checks what is inside before it installs anything, and asks before it turns off another theme.

  • Download the archiveIt lands in your Downloads folder.
  • Copy the promptIt tells Claude Code what to check and where to unpack.
  • Paste it into Claude CodeIn the Claude desktop app. It installs the theme and tells you when to reload.

Can I make my own?


  

    
winch-ai

    
Yes, in the editor. Start from one of these themes, your own picture or a blank scene, then stack layers, paint cell by cell, add motion and give every colour its time of day. It checks the scene against the app's player as you work, and exports the same archive and install prompt as the themes here.

    
Open the editorIt runs in your browser, and your work stays on your computer.
